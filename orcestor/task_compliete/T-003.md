**Maqsad (Goal):**
`src/app/features/dashboard/pages/dashboard/dashboard.css` (va zarur
bo'lsa `dashboard.html`dagi struktura o'zgarmasdan, faqat class
qo'shish) faylini `project_docs/design-reference.md` bo'lim 3 "Stat
karta" qismiga mos holda qayta stillashtirish.

`dashboard.ts` allaqachon 4 ta REAL statistikani hisoblaydi (`Jami
