**Maqsad (Goal):**
`core`, `auth`, `dashboard`, `company`, `branches`, `departments`,
`positions` qismlarining barcha API "point"larini (endpoint'larini),
so'rov/javob (request/response) tiplarini, VA har bir sahifa

ichiga har bir modul uchun alohida `.md` fayl qilib hujjatlashtirish —
review'ni osonlashtirish uchun.

**Kontekst (Context):**
- Barcha HTTP so'rovlar yagona `src/app/core/services/http.ts` (`Http`
  klassi) orqali o'tadi — `get<T>`, `post<T>`, `put<T>`, `patch<T>`,
  `delete<T>` generic metodlari bor, baza URL `environment.apiUrl`.
- Har bir feature'ning `services/*.ts` fayli shu `Http`dan foydalanib
  o'ziga xos endpoint'larni chaqiradi (masalan `branches/services/branch.ts`
  ichida `this.http.get<Branch[]>('/branches')` kabi).
- Endpoint yo'llari hozircha **taxminiy/placeholder** — haqiqiy backend yo'q,
  shuning uchun bu hujjat "frontend nima kutayotganini" rasmiylashtiradi
  (kelajakda backend yozilganda shartnoma sifatida ishlatiladi).
- Tiplar `src/app/core/models/*.ts` va har bir feature'ning ichida
  ta'riflangan bo'lishi mumkin.
- Holat (state) boshqaruvi loyihada izchil emas — ba'zi sahifalar oddiy
  class property ishlatadi (masalan `branches: Branch[] = []`), ba'zilari
  Angular `signal()` ishlatadi (masalan `login.ts`, `settings-page.ts`).
  Buni ham aniq ro'yxatga oling — qaysi yondashuv ishlatilgani.
